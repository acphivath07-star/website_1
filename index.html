03 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Can I save any YouTube video?

Clipnest is designed for videos you own or have permission to use. For YouTube content, use the creator's official download tools or Google Takeout.

Do you keep my files forever?

No. Approved files can use temporary download links, while you control where your long-term originals live.

What links are supported?

The first version is intended for direct links from storage providers you control, such as S3, Cloudinary, or another approved file host.
